# Музыка для ASCII / Седой Бор

## 1. Главное меню — «Бледное стекло»

Minimal dark ambient menu music for a melancholic ASCII fantasy exploration game, 64 BPM. Sparse felt piano, delicate glass chimes, soft cello harmonics, a restrained low drone. A memorable four-note motif, long quiet pauses, faded gold and cold green atmosphere. Intimate, mysterious, quietly inviting. Instrumental, clean recording, no vocals, no drums, no hiss or crackle. Seamless two-minute loop, balanced volume, no dramatic climax.

## 2. Город-крепость — «Седой Бор»

Understated medieval village ambience, 74 BPM, for a small fortress town surrounded by grain fields. Soft plucked lute, wooden flute, a warm viola drone, occasional muted frame-drum taps. A modest folk melody that feels lived-in and slightly weary; safe shelter with distant unease. Instrumental, dry and intimate, no epic choir, no modern synth lead, no hiss. Seamless three-minute loop; leave space for dialogue and footsteps.

## 3. Лес и аномалии — «Следы в долине»

Quiet uncanny forest score for an ASCII dark-fantasy game, 52 BPM. Bowed strings, breathy low flute, isolated detuned bell tones and a deep, gently evolving drone. Small repeating patterns drift out of alignment, then return; tension comes from silence and unresolved harmony. Curious and unsettling, never aggressive. Instrumental, no vocals, no jump scares, no heavy percussion, no noise bed or hiss. Seamless three-minute exploration loop with restrained dynamics.
